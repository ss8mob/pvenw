PVE Neighborhood Watch - Street Meet

What Is a Street Meet?

A Street Meet is an informal, one-hour gathering of neighbors in a cul-de-sac, driveway, or front yard. It provides an opportunity for neighbors to meet one another, connect with PVE Police Department officers and PVE Neighborhood Watch volunteers, and learn more about keeping their homes, cars, and neighborhood safe. Topics may include current crime trends, crime prevention, emergency preparedness, and the importance of working together during an emergency or disaster. Most importantly, a Street Meet is a chance to build relationships with your neighbors before you need them.
